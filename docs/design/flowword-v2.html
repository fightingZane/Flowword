The little details that make a difference

Good design is often a series of small decisions. The new frame feels more balanced, while the camera system takes a different approach.

Instead of adding more components, the team chose to take a step back and rethink the entire structure.

Sometimes, the simplest solution is the one that lets you see the bigger picture.
